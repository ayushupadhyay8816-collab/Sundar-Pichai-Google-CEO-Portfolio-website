A journey from studying engineering in India to leading one of the world’s best-known technology companies.

PRODUCT · PEOPLE · PROGRESS

Sundar Pichai, born Pichai Sundararajan in Chennai, India, developed an interest in technology and engineering before building an international career. He studied Metallurgical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, then continued his education at Stanford University and the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania.

He joined Google in 2004 and helped lead work on Google Toolbar and Google Chrome. His responsibilities later expanded across major products and platforms, including Search, Android, Maps, Gmail and Google Play. He became Google’s CEO in August 2015 and Alphabet’s CEO in December 2019.

His career highlights how engineering knowledge, product thinking, teamwork and continuous learning can contribute to leadership in a fast-changing industry.
